# Offline retrospective H6 pilot — protocol 1.0.0

Protocol: offline-retro-h6-1.0.0-pilot. Observational pilot; NOT PREREGISTERED. Activation for newly configured studies is recorded in activation.json after validation. Existing M0 studies retain their protocol, consent, questionnaires, assignments and source answers. No automatic enrollment migration is permitted.

The primary Random/v0.3/PED collection-day-slot experiment, M0 eligibility/window mapping and frozen implementations are unchanged. Every H6 envelope contains an independently valid M0 v1.0.0 core. Its two exact neutral questions and atomic neutral lock remain normative. The H6 core permits no legacy optional questions. H6 follow-ups are released only after that durable lock, with a total cap of two across the interview.

Bank h6-followups-1.0.0 covers context, skipping and moment-level reports. Policy h6-context-plus-balanced-domain-1.0.0 offers context plus one seed/episode-hash-selected skip/moment domain for CLEAR/VAGUE recognition, context only for uncertain/non-recall states, and none for DECLINED. It does not use arm, meaningfulness score, predicted motive or psychological label. Eligible/selected/not-selected/ineligible IDs are persisted at neutral lock; exact released question definitions/options and timestamps, immutable submissions and completion status distinguish shown/skipped/answered/uncertain responses. Shown denotes release to the bundled local interview UI; it does not prove attention or reading.

H6 collection requires its own explicit consent. Sharing extended events/sessions, playlist context and follow-up evidence are separate fixed choices. Playlist context requires extended-event sharing; extended events require core listening-history sharing. Unselected categories are omitted, never synthesized or hidden in native detector payloads. Responses-only bundles contain no source-event or session extension, and enumerate unavailable analyses. Full preview and final explicit authorization cover both core and supplement. Declining sharing does not retroactively erase local answers; local deletion removes all study data under the existing withdrawal rules.

Provider-native values are allowlisted and preserved with normalized representations. Unknown skip remains UNKNOWN. ms_played is not recording duration, continuous exposure or a listening position. Provider navigation and trackdone describe reported actions/termination, not intention, preference or enjoyment. Sessionization uses provider outcome times, a 30-minute gap, and deterministic source-ordinal/event-key tie breaks; uncertain starts remain uncertain. Complete ordered membership and transition evidence is exported when authorized, with versioned parameters and input commitment.

Participant-described contexts and musical/lyrical moments are reports, not externally verified facts. Optional positions/intervals are approximate and do not establish measured exposure. H6 collection produces no psychological classification, latent motivational label, LLM investigation or DSP-derived explanation. A retention reversal does not establish a psychological function or validate H6.

The authoritative instrument, consent, policy, schemas, validators, canonical hashes and archive codec are distributed together. The private receiver validates the complete envelope and stores source records exactly; core materialization remains diagnostic and never replaces source assignments or answers. Old M0 readers reject H6 containers; the new dual-version reader continues to accept old valid M0 captures. External metadata acquisition is not part of participant collection.
